# Security Policy

LiveSeed takes the security of our software products, repositories, and developer community seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose vulnerabilities to help us keep our ecosystem safe.

---

## Supported Versions

Only the latest active major/minor release of each project receives active security updates and patches:

| Project / Version | Supported          |
| :---------------- | :----------------- |
| `main` / `latest` | :white_check_mark: |
| `< 1.0.0` (Alpha) | :x:                |

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.**

If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability or security risk in any LiveSeed repository:

1. **GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting (Preferred):**
   - Navigate to the affected repository's **Security** tab.
   - Click **Report a vulnerability** under the Advisories section to open a confidential report directly with our maintainers.

2. **Direct Email:**
   - Send details to **security@liveseed.in** (or **contact@liveseed.in**).
   - Please include:
     - Type and severity of the issue.
     - Affected repository, branch, or release version.
     - Step-by-step instructions or a minimal Proof of Concept (PoC) to reproduce the issue.
     - Any potential mitigations or workarounds you have identified.

---

## Our Response Commitment

- **Acknowledgment:** We aim to acknowledge receipt of vulnerability reports within **48 hours**.
- **Assessment:** We will validate the issue and determine its impact and severity within **5 business days**.
- **Resolution:** A fix will be developed, tested, and released as quickly as possible, coordinated with a public advisory and appropriate credit.

---

## Responsible Disclosure & Safe Harbor

We ask that you:
- Provide us a reasonable amount of time to resolve the issue before disclosing it publicly.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or destroying user data or violating any privacy rights during your research.
- Conduct testing only within your own environments or test accounts.

Activities conducted in good faith under these principles will be considered authorized and will not result in legal action.
